01 — The Movement

Equality is not a destination.
It is a journey.

Women's empowerment is not about giving women special treatment. It is about creating a society where every woman has the freedom, opportunity and confidence to learn, work, lead, make decisions and live with dignity.

02 — Foundations

Rights are the foundation of freedom.

Tap each right to learn more.

01

Education

Equal access to quality education, from primary school through higher learning.

02

Work

Equal opportunities to build careers and participate fully in the workforce.

03

Equal Pay

Fair compensation for work of equal value, regardless of gender.

04

Safety

Freedom from violence, harassment and discrimination, at home and in public life.

05

Property

Equal rights to own, inherit and control property and assets.

06

Political Participation

The right to vote, participate in public life and lead.

07

Freedom

The ability to make decisions about education, career, movement and life.

04 — Understanding

What does feminism really mean?

Feminism is the belief that women and men should have equal rights, opportunities, dignity and freedom.

FEMINISM = HATRED
FEMINISM = EQUALITY
Equal education
Equal opportunities
Economic independence
Political participation
Freedom from discrimination
Safety and dignity

05 — A Century of Voices

A century of voices.

1848

Seneca Falls Convention launches the modern women's rights movement.

1945

The UN Charter recognizes the equal rights of men and women.

1979

CEDAW — the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women — is adopted.

1995

The Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action is adopted.

2015

SDG 5 — Gender Equality — is set as a global goal.

TODAY

Women continue to reshape leadership, science, technology, business and society.

08 — Women Who Rose

They did not wait for the world to change.

Savitribai Phule

Education & Social Reform

Pioneered girls' education in 19th-century India, opening schools that challenged deep social barriers.

Kalpana Chawla

Space & STEM

The first Indian-born woman in space, she inspired a generation of women in science and engineering.

Mary Kom

Sports

A six-time world boxing champion who redefined what was possible for women in competitive sport.

Falguni Nayar

Entrepreneurship

Built one of India's largest beauty and retail platforms, becoming a self-made billionaire founder.

Droupadi Murmu

Political Leadership

Rose from a tribal community to become President of India, a landmark moment for representation.

Sudha Murty

Education & Philanthropy

An engineer and philanthropist whose foundations have expanded access to education across India.
